What problems does it try to solve?

Stress. Students feel overwhelmed, mentally tired and under academic pressure, and often cannot reach support easily.

What is it?

NeuroCalm Access is a BrainTech project concept. It explores how technology and neuroscience could make calming and stress-management support more accessible and personal.

What is the hypothesis?

Tracking mood, practicing guided breathing and using interactive focus tools will reduce stress and improve concentration for students during high-stress times.

Why this design?

Research suggests colors affect mood, and blue, green and yellow are linked with feeling relaxed and focused. That is why they run through the whole site.

Research published by the National Institutes of Health suggests breathing exercises can help reduce stress, so we added breathing tips.

How will we test it?

1. Gather scientific information on neurofeedback, stress-relief techniques and focus methods.

2. Run a feedback survey with students and peers.

3. Use the feedback to improve features, accessibility and effectiveness.

This is a school prototype, not medical advice. If you feel very low, talk to someone you trust.
